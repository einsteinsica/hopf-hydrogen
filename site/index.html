Hydrogen Orbital Structure from Harmonic Analysis on S³

2026

We show that every hydrogen orbital shape emerges from harmonic analysis on the 3-sphere S³. An ergodic trajectory on S³ (generated here by composing three SU(2) rotations with Z₃ cyclic symmetry) is weighted by eigenfunctions of the Laplacian ∆S³. Each eigenfunction selects a different orbital: s, p, d, and f, for all principal quantum numbers n. No Schrödinger equation or Coulomb potential is used. Three results go beyond Fock's 1935 momentum-space correspondence: (i) the shell capacity 2n² follows from the eigenspace dimension (k+1)² at Laplacian degree k = n−1; (ii) the Aufbau energy ordering follows from the strictly increasing eigenvalue sequence −k(k+2): 0, 3, 8, 15, …; (iii) the radial wavefunctions are Gegenbauer polynomials on the compact interval [0, π], rather than Laguerre polynomials on [0, ∞) — both give n−l−1 radial nodes, but the node positions differ due to S³ compactification, yielding a falsifiable prediction. An interactive WebGPU demonstration accompanies this paper. The orbital shape is not imposed — it is inevitable geometry.

Key Result

All 20 hydrogen orbitals (n = 1 through 4) emerge from the same quark rotation path on S³. Only the harmonic filter changes. The electron traverses a single ergodic trajectory; the weight function |YN(y)|² evaluated at each S³ point determines which regions contribute to the visible probability cloud. Quantum numbers (n, l, m) are not inputs — they are eigenvalue labels of the S³ Laplacian.

Construction

Three SU(2) paths with Z₃ cyclic frequency permutation:

qR(t) = normalize(cos ω₁t, sin ω₂t, cos ω₃t, sin ω₄t)
qG(t) = normalize(cos ω₂t, sin ω₃t, cos ω₄t, sin ω₁t)
qB(t) = normalize(cos ω₃(−t), sin ω₄(−t), cos ω₁(−t), sin ω₂(−t))

Electron: q(t) = qR · qG · qB — SU(2) group multiplication. The orbit is dense in S³ when frequencies are mutually incommensurate.
