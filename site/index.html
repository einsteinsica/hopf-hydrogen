Emergent Hydrogen Orbitals from Quark Rotation Geometry on S³

2026

We show that every hydrogen orbital shape emerges from a single geometric construction on the 3-sphere S³. Three quarks rotate independently through S³ via SU(2) paths with Z₃ cyclic frequency permutation. The electron's trajectory is the composed rotation q = qR · qG · qB. When this trajectory is weighted by eigenfunctions of the Laplacian on S³ — Gegenbauer polynomials and their angular counterparts — every hydrogen orbital appears: s, p, d, and f, for all principal quantum numbers n. No Schrödinger equation or Coulomb potential is used. The shell capacity 2n² and subshell capacity 2(2l+1) follow from the eigenvalue degeneracy of ∆S³. An interactive WebGPU demonstration accompanies this paper, showing the probability clouds building in real time from the quark path. The orbital shape is not imposed — it is inevitable geometry.

Key Result

All 20 hydrogen orbitals (n = 1 through 4) emerge from the same quark rotation path on S³. Only the harmonic filter changes. The electron traverses a single ergodic trajectory; the weight function |YN(y)|² evaluated at each S³ point determines which regions contribute to the visible probability cloud. Quantum numbers (n, l, m) are not inputs — they are eigenvalue labels of the S³ Laplacian.

Construction

Three SU(2) paths with Z₃ cyclic frequency permutation:

qR(t) = normalize(cos ω₁t, sin ω₂t, cos ω₃t, sin ω₄t)
qG(t) = normalize(cos ω₂t, sin ω₃t, cos ω₄t, sin ω₁t)
qB(t) = normalize(cos ω₃(−t), sin ω₄(−t), cos ω₁(−t), sin ω₂(−t))

Electron: q(t) = qR · qG · qB — SU(2) group multiplication. The orbit is dense in S³ when frequencies are mutually incommensurate.
